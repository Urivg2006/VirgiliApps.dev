# Decimal Dates ⏱️

Turn any date into **decimal years** instantly.

Add a date (birthday, milestone, event) and the app tells you how many years have passed or how many are left, with the number of decimals you choose.

```
Birthday         12/03/2014  →  12.71 years elapsed
Trip             01/07/2027  →  −0.60 years remaining
```

## Features

- **Events** with a name, date and optional time.
- **Decimal years** with the precision you choose, from 1 to 8 decimals.
- **Automatic updates** at the optimal interval for the selected precision.
- **Local reminders** when the date arrives and every year (with system permission).
- **Three home screen widget styles**, resizable, with colours for the past and the future.
- **Light and dark mode**, including the widget.
- **20 languages**, switchable without restarting the app.
- **Side drawer** with every option.

## Privacy

Dates are stored **only on your device**. The app has no accounts and no servers of its own, and collects no personal data.
